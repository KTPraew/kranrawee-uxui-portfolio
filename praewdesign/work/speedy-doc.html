Case Study

Speedy Doc

Turning a scattered filing system into one place teams could actually search and trust.

Role
Sole UX/UI Designer, working alongside a PM and developers
Timeline
1-2 months
Year
2025
Tools
  • Figma

Problem

People across government and private organizations were losing hours hunting for documents scattered across different storage locations. Physical records carried their own security risks, and every minute spent searching was a minute not spent on the actual work.

Process

  1. 01

    Discover

    Research, user interviews, personas, and competitive analysis

  2. 02

    Define

    Card sorting, feature prioritization, and journey mapping

  3. 03

    Develop

    Information architecture, wireframes, prototyping, and usability testing

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Final UI design and visual mockups across desktop, tablet, and mobile

Key Decisions

Centralized into one system

I centralized everything into one system instead of leaving documents split across departments, so people had one place to search instead of many.

Permission-based sharing and version tracking

I added permission-based sharing and version tracking, since security and losing track of the "latest" file were two of the biggest complaints.

Soft-delete recovery

I built in soft-delete recovery, so an accidental delete wouldn't mean permanently losing a document.

Solution

Reflection

This project did include a round of usability testing, though not with measurable results to report here. What stayed with me most was how much friction disappeared just by giving people one place to search, and how much small trust-building details mattered: version history, permissions, and the ability to recover something deleted by mistake.
