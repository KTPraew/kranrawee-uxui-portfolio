Case Study

C-HAT CRM

Helping beauty salon owners remember and win back the customers who matter most.

Role
Sole UX/UI Designer, working alongside a PM and developers
Timeline
2 months
Year
2025
Tools
  • Figma

Problem

Salon and lash studio owners knew their regulars by face, but couldn't keep track of everyone else. Without that memory, promotions rarely reached the right people, and repeat business slipped through the cracks.

I can't remember all my customers' details. I mostly remember the ones who come in often.

— Salon owner

Process

  1. 01

    Discover

    SWOT analysis, plus interviews with 2 salon owners

  2. 02

    Define

    Mapped customer interaction scenarios through storyboarding

  3. 03

    Design

    Built the information architecture, then high-fidelity wireframes in Figma across three connected surfaces: a customer-facing LINE app (LIFF), a LINE OA CRM for staff, and a web back-office

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Refined layouts, simplified the language for non-technical staff, and added a collapsible menu to keep three platforms feeling manageable

Key Decisions

Three connected surfaces

I built across three connected surfaces instead of one, because staff, customers, and back-office admins each needed their own entry point into the same data.

Simplified language

I simplified the language throughout. Most salon staff weren't used to CRM software, so the interface needed to feel familiar, not technical.

Consistent brand color palette

I applied the brand's color palette consistently across all three surfaces, so the tools felt like one product instead of three separate ones.

Solution

Reflection

What I learned

This project didn't include a formal testing round, so instead of results, here's what I took away. Two honest conversations with business owners told me more than a long survey would have.

What I'd validate next

If I got to revisit this, I'd want to validate two things: whether staff actually use the customer notes feature day-to-day, and whether the simplified language still holds up once real customer data fills the screen instead of placeholder content.
