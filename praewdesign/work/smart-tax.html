Case Study

Smart Tax Platform

Helping government officials verify property records without resurveying everything by hand.

Role
Sole UX/UI Designer, working alongside a PM and developers
Timeline
1 year
Year
2024
Tools
  • Figma
  • React Ant Design

Problem

Property tax records covering land, buildings, signage, and condos went out of date quickly, and rechecking them meant resurveying almost everything by hand. The process was inconsistent from one staff member to another, and even updating basic information was its own struggle.

Process

  1. 01

    Empathize

    Consulted domain experts and interviewed field staff to understand daily survey workflows and pain points

  2. 02

    Define

    Mapped out problem statements and business goals from what surfaced in those conversations

  3. 03

    Ideate & Design

    Explored solution concepts, then built user flows and interface designs for a mobile survey tool

  4. 04

    Test

    Validated the design before handoff

Key Decisions

AI for signage verification

I brought AI into the survey flow specifically for asset types like signage, where verification was the most repetitive and error-prone part of the job.

Mobile-first design

I designed mobile-first, since officials do most of their survey work out in the field, not at a desk.

Built on React Ant Design

I built on React Ant Design to keep the interface consistent with other internal government tools, so officials wouldn't need to learn a new visual language.

Solution

Reflection

This project didn't include a testing round with measurable results, so here's what stayed with me instead. I learned more about how property tax actually works than I expected to, which made it easier to design for the reality of the job rather than my assumptions about it. Working across a full year also meant managing a flexible, sometimes uneven schedule, and revisiting earlier decisions through retrospectives whenever something didn't sit right.
