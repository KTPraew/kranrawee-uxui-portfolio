C-HAT CRM

Customer Relationship Management

About CRM

C-HAT had already been developed, but no customers were using it. The target customers were local shops. That led to a new system based on the original concept but more specific: a CRM focused on beauty businesses such as nail salons and eyelash extension shops.

I designed a flow for their main pain point, appointment booking. The main features built to meet user needs are appointment booking and special privileges.

Role
UX/UI Designer
Tool
Figma
Year
2025
Project time
2 months

Problem

  • Booking appointments is a main pain point for these businesses.
  • Stores don't know who their customers are.
  • They can't remember customer details.
  • They can't retain existing customers.
  • Promotions are not reaching or being noticed by customers.

Solution

  • Centralize customer data: Collect all customer information in one place.
  • Easily reach target customers: Access specific customer segments with ease.
  • Encourage customer retention: Incentivize repeat purchases from existing customers.
  • Enhance customer satisfaction: Make booking and special privileges easier for customers.
  • Drive business growth: Streamline operations for business owners, marketing, and sales teams.

Research Goal

To identify the specific pain points and challenges local shops face in managing customer data.

SWOT analysis

I created a SWOT analysis to look at internal and external factors that could affect the viability of the interface.

Strengths

  • Agile and adaptable
  • Fresh perspective
  • Niche market
  • Strong customer service
  • Flexible operations

Weaknesses

  • Data privacy
  • Data security
  • Cost of implementation
  • Limited technical expertise
  • Data integration
  • Scalability

Opportunities

  • Customer retention
  • Increased sales
  • Improved customer satisfaction
  • Targeted marketing
  • Operational efficiency
  • Decision support

Threats

  • Economic conditions

Interviews
