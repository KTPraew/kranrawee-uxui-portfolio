Document management system

Discover

Problem assumption

Initial research showed that users often cannot find their documents.

Why:

  • There are too many documents and it's difficult to find them.
  • The documents are disorganized and scattered in different places.

“ปกติเอกสารหรือหนังสือสำคัญก็อยู่กับคนที่เกี่ยวข้องกับงานนั้นๆ ถ้าจะใช้ก็ถามๆกันเอาเอง มีเจอบ้าง ไม่เจอบ้าง”

Usually, important documents stay with whoever works on that task. When we need one, we just ask around. Sometimes we find it, sometimes we don't.

K. Kanokwan

Define

Problem

  • Difficulty accessing critical documents
  • Large number of documents, time-consuming to find, and disorganized
  • Security risks associated with physical documents
  • Decreased productivity due to time wasted searching for documents

Expected benefits

  • Reduced costs
  • Reduced environmental impact due to decreased paper consumption

Insight

  • Inefficiency
    Document handling is inefficient. Searching takes a long time and storage is disorganized.
  • Higher costs
    Paper and ink use raises operating costs.
  • Lack of organization
    Documents are not well organized, so specific information is hard to find.
  • Risk of errors
    A disorganized system makes it easier to lose or misfile important documents.
  • Lower productivity
    Time spent searching and fixing document issues reduces employee productivity.

Solve the problem

  • Keep all documents in one place so they are easy to find.
  • Share files securely, only with the right people.
  • Track changes to files over time.
  • Find files fast with powerful search.
  • Offer soft delete so accidentally deleted files can be recovered.
